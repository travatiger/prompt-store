--- New in this build: Marketplace / Sellers ---
Any signed-in user can now become a seller instantly from Account → 💼 Sell
(no approval needed to start). They set their own display name and their own
payment info (e.g. their own Binance UID) — buyers pay them DIRECTLY, not you.
Sellers manage their own products (add/edit/deactivate/delete, with image and
video uploads) and approve/reject their own buyers' payment requests, since
only they can see whether the money actually arrived in their account.

You (the site admin) keep full control:
 - Admin → 🧑‍💼 Sellers: see every seller, their payment info, how many
   products they have, and how much commission they currently owe you. Ban
   a seller at any time — this immediately hides all of their listings.
 - A platform-wide commission percentage (default 10%) is editable from the
   same tab. It's recorded automatically on every seller sale for
   bookkeeping. Because payment goes straight from buyer to seller (no
   payment gateway in this system), the commission is NOT auto-deducted —
   you collect it from the seller separately, then mark each sale's
   commission as "paid" (this granular per-order toggle isn't in the UI yet;
   ask me to add it if you want it exposed as a button).
 - Your existing admin-only product catalog is untouched and still works
   exactly as before — seller products just live alongside it, clearly
   marked "(seller-owned)" in the admin Products tab.

PLEASE NOTE: your original schema.sql (create_order, submit_payment,
get_my_prompt, review_payment, is_admin) was not included in this package,
so the seller/commission fields on `orders` are filled automatically by a
database trigger rather than by editing those functions — this was the
safest way to add the feature without seeing their exact original logic.
If your real create_order/submit_payment do anything beyond what's described
above, let me know and I'll adjust the trigger to match.
